Mission setup

This version works for one learner or for one device shared by a team of four.

The timer is a pacing tool. When it reaches zero, the game remains usable so students can finish rather than lose work.

Team roles

For teams of four, assign these verbally before starting.

Time Detective — establishes the time relationship.
Aspect Detective — decides completed / ongoing / repeated / prior.
Grammar Lawyer — explains the choice.
Challenger — tests alternative interpretations.

Clues, not rules: since/for → often perfect; by + time → often perfect; this time tomorrow → often future continuous; finished past time → usually past simple.
